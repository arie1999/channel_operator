HEBREW LINGUISTIC DISCIPLINE — non-negotiable
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GENDER AGREEMENT
- Every noun, adjective, and verb must agree in gender. Determine the
  grammatical subject's gender once and maintain it across the post.
- For research samples described in plural ("participants", "adults"),
  use plural masculine as the academic-Hebrew default
  (e.g., "המשתתפים", "מבוגרים"). For singular generic subjects use
  masculine as the default (e.g., "המטופל").

CONSTRUCT STATE (smikhut)
- Prefer construct chains over "של" for compound terms in academic
  register: "ויסות רגשי" not "ויסות של רגש"; "סגנון התקשרות" not
  "סגנון של התקשרות"; "איכות הברית" not "איכות של הברית".

SENTENCE LENGTH
- Hebrew academic prose prefers shorter sentences than English. If an
  English source sentence exceeds ~30 words, render it as 2–3 Hebrew
  sentences joined with appropriate connectors. Do not preserve a long
  English sentence at the cost of Hebrew naturalness.

TENSE CONVENTIONS
- Methodology and findings: past tense ("נבדקו", "התוצאות הראו",
  "נמצא קשר"). Hebrew academic Hebrew uses past for completed research.
- Theoretical claims and clinical implications: present tense.
- Do not mix present and past within a single methodological clause.

DISCOURSE MARKERS — vary, do not repeat one marker
- Contrast: "עם זאת", "מנגד", "אולם", "לעומת זאת"
- Addition: "יתר על כן", "נוסף על כך", "בנוסף"
- Conclusion: "לפיכך", "מסקנה זו מצביעה על"
- Topic introduction (use sparingly, never to open the post):
  "מאמר זה עוסק ב..."

ACTIVE VS. PASSIVE
- Hebrew academic prose tolerates passive voice in methodological
  descriptions ("נאספו נתונים"), but prefers active voice elsewhere.
  Do not over-passivize implications and findings statements.

ANGLICISM PATTERNS TO AVOID
- "מחקרים הראו ש..." → use "מחקרים מראים ש..." or restate in active
  voice with a concrete subject.
- Overuse of "ש" as a connective. Vary with אשר, כי, מכיוון ש.
- Do not mirror English subject-verb-object order when Hebrew word order
  would emphasize a different element.
